# Deal Blast Pro - 5-Minute Investor Demo Script

**Target Audience:** Potential investors, partners, or serious enterprise prospects.

**Total Time:** 5 minutes (tight, high-signal)

## Script Structure

### 0:00 – 0:30 | Landing & Problem
- Open `/`
- "This is the public face of Deal Blast Pro."
- Highlight headline: "Organize Every Deal. Match Every Buyer. Follow Up Until It Closes."
- Quickly show Problem → Solution framing on the page.

### 0:30 – 1:00 | Pricing (Credibility)
- Go to `/pricing`
- "We have a clear, tiered model from Free Demo all the way to Enterprise."
- Point out the **Pro** tier as the sweet spot for active users.
- "This is not a toy — this is how professional dispo teams will actually pay for the platform."

### 1:00 – 1:45 | Public Portal (Intake)
- Go to `/portal`
- "Anyone can submit a deal through this public portal — no login required."
- "The 6-step wizard is the same one our internal users use."
- Click into the wizard briefly to show depth (don't complete it).

### 1:45 – 3:00 | Live Dashboard & Core Loop (The Money Shot)
- Login using **"Use Demo Login"**
- Immediately click **"Run Sample Workflow"** (biggest value moment)
- Once the deal opens in the terminal:
  - Show **Buyer Match** tab with live scores and response tracking
  - Show **Blast Builder** — select buyers + documents → Send blast
  - Show blast log immediately appear in Activity
  - Open **Pipeline Board** — show Kanban and quick status change
  - Open **Analytics** — show real response rates and heat scores

**Key line:** "Everything you just saw — matching, blasting, follow-up, pipeline, analytics — is one operating system. Not five spreadsheets."

### 3:00 – 3:45 | Follow-Up Center + Automation
- Go to Follow-Up Task Center
- Show scheduled follow-ups and the email generator
- "This is where most teams lose deals. We make it impossible to forget."

### 3:45 – 4:30 | Settings & Backend Readiness (Credibility)
- Go to Settings
- Show:
  - Demo Readiness Checklist
  - Storage Mode & Adapter Health (shows we're architected for real backend)
  - Developer Handoff Checklist + full documentation in `/docs`
- "This is not a prototype. This is a production-ready foundation with a clear path to real multi-tenant backend."

### 4:30 – 5:00 | Close
- "We built the hardest parts first: the 6-step wizard, weighted matching engine, per-deal suppression, blast logging, heat scores, and pipeline."
- "The question is no longer *if* this works — it's how fast we can get it in front of real users."
- Offer: "I can show you the full 12-minute technical demo or walk through the investor data room if you'd like."

- Settings "Clear All Data" unless you're resetting
