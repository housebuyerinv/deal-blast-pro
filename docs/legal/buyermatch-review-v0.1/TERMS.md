## 2. Plans and limits
Starter: USD59 per month, 20 analyses, 10 software distributions and up to 25 buyers
per distribution. Pro: USD119 per month, 50 analyses, 25 software distributions and
up to 50 buyers per distribution. One subscription quantity; customer-selected
quantity multiplication is unavailable. These limits describe software allowances,
not a promise of that many eligible buyers, delivered messages or responses.

## 4. Buyer privacy and authorized use
Use only deal and contact information you have authority to supply and process.
Do not attempt to identify hidden buyers, scrape the network, transfer response
capabilities, bypass workspace controls, send unlawful messages or override opt-outs.
Private buyer identity is controlled through the approved introduction workflow.
Deal authority, applicable consent and any deal-specific agreements remain required
before distribution. Platform acceptance is not blanket recipient marketing consent.

## 5. Outcomes, data and professional review

funding, sale, title clearance or closing. Independently verify property, financial
and third-party information. Software is not a substitute for professional advice.
