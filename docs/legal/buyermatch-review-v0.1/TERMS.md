## 2. Plans and limits
Starter: USD59 per month, 20 analyses, 10 software distributions and up to 25 buyers
per distribution, with a maximum of 250 invitations per monthly allowance period.
Pro: USD119 per month, 50 analyses, 25 software distributions and up to 50 buyers
per distribution, with a maximum of 1,250 invitations per monthly allowance period.
Analysis requests are additionally limited to 10 analyses in a rolling hour.
One subscription quantity; customer-selected
quantity multiplication is unavailable. These limits describe software allowances,
not a promise of that many eligible buyers, delivered messages or responses.

## 3. Billing and access
Monthly recurring service; see the separate proposed Renewal/Cancellation and Refund

Existing CRM access remains governed by its independent subscription.

[OWNER DECISION: payment-failure grace period, retry/dunning notices and the exact

## 4. Buyer privacy and authorized use
Use only deal and contact information you have authority to supply and process.
Do not attempt to identify hidden buyers, scrape the network, transfer response
capabilities, bypass workspace controls, send unlawful messages or override opt-outs.
Private buyer identity is controlled through the approved introduction workflow.
Deal authority, applicable consent and any deal-specific agreements remain required
before distribution. Platform acceptance is not blanket recipient marketing consent.

## 5. Outcomes, data and professional review
Matching describes criteria fit, not a probability or guarantee of buyer response,
funding, sale, title clearance or closing. Independently verify property, financial
and third-party information. Software is not a substitute for professional advice.
[COUNSEL REVIEW: appropriate disclaimers, availability commitments, liability,

## 6. Property conversion records (review addition)
BuyerMatch can report recorded property/transaction stages, matches, recipient
delivery, responses, offers, earnest-money evidence, outcomes and fee records.
Some facts come from existing application records; others are customer-entered
attestations with references. A recorded offer or receipt is not independent
document authentication, escrow confirmation, bank reconciliation or proof that
funds moved. Projected and invoiced fees are separate from recorded receipts.
Recording a fee does not collect it or establish a right to compensation.

Unresolved property identities and unknown amounts are labeled. Duplicate
association requires review and retains original submissions and correction
history. Cohort/window denominators, missing observations and non-linear stage
history limit interpretation; analytics are not predicted closing probabilities,
investment advice, an appraisal or a guarantee of transaction performance.
