# Proposed BuyerMatch Refund and Billing-Dispute Policy
Version: buyermatch-review-v0.1-refund | Prepared: 2026-10-23
**DRAFT — NOT APPROVED — NOT FOR PUBLICATION OR ACCEPTANCE**

Seller/contact/effective date: [OWNER VERIFY]. This draft concerns independent
BuyerMatch software only. CRM subscriptions, JV agreements and managed services
require their own applicable terms; avoid conflicting cross-product promises.

[PROPOSED DEFAULT — OWNER/COUNSEL APPROVAL REQUIRED] Subscription charges are
nonrefundable except where applicable law requires otherwise or an approved written
exception applies. This mirrors the generic DBP policy's starting position, not a
new determination that it is appropriate or enforceable for BuyerMatch.

[OWNER/COUNSEL DECISIONS]
- Required statutory rights and markets; whether any refund window or discretionary
  satisfaction policy is offered.
- Duplicate/incorrect charges, outages, unavailable software and unused allowances.
- Cancellation during a paid period; proration credits versus cash refunds.
- Refund processing method/time expectations and effect on paid access.
- Fraud/unauthorized payment reports, dispute response and appropriate account
  restrictions while a charge is reviewed.
- Evidence requested without collecting full payment-card credentials or unnecessary
  private buyer/customer information.

[PROPOSED PROCESS — OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED] Customers contact the approved
BuyerMatch support address with account identity and the relevant invoice reference.
Staff verify ownership privately and explain the decision and any access effect.
Do not ask for card numbers, passwords or response capability tokens by email.
[OWNER VERIFY: support address, response SLA and escalation/appeal route.]

A successful payment does not guarantee matches or a closing; an absence of matches
must be addressed under the final approved software policy rather than an invented
guarantee. Nothing in this draft approves a charge, refund, dispute action or live
billing change. Keep final terms, portal behavior and webhook refund/access rules
consistent and test the approved scenarios before public launch.
